Run thousands of possible futures before you put money in.

Read this first: every number here comes from how the stock moved in the past. It can't see tomorrow's news, results or market crashes, and nothing can promise a profit. Use it to understand the range of what can happen and size your risk. It isn't financial advice.

Get real NSE data

  1. Tap a stock below to open its NSE page.
  2. Scroll to Historical Data, choose 1Y, then tap Download (.csv).
  3. Come back here, tap Upload CSV files and pick all the downloaded files at once.

Want 2 years? Download two 1-year ranges for the same stock and upload both. They're merged automatically. NSE's site works best in a phone browser in desktop mode, or on a computer.

Or add a stock by hand (no history needed)

Hand-added stocks can be simulated but not backtested or ranked, because there's no price history. Typical yearly volatility: large caps 20–30%, mid caps 30–45%, small caps 45%+.

Which stock looks best right now?

Ranks your loaded stocks on trend, momentum per unit of risk, and overbought/oversold readings, then simulates a trade plan for each: entry at the last close, stop 1.5 daily ranges below, target 2 daily ranges above.

Best time of day to buy and sell?

Upload a 5- or 15-minute candle CSV (at least 20 trading days; 60+ is better). The tool finds the buy-time / sell-time pair that worked best on the first 70% of days, then checks it on the last 30% it never saw. If the pattern disappears on the unseen days, it was noise.

Everything runs on this page; uploaded files stay on your device. Past behaviour doesn't guarantee future results.
